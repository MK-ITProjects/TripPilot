Privacy Policy

Placeholder privacy notes for this college demo project.

This project stores only what is needed to demonstrate its features: your account details (username, email, hashed password), any trip plans you create, and reviews you leave on destinations.

What is stored
  • Account info you provide at signup (username, name, email)
  • Trip plans, saved stops and reviews you create while signed in
  • Session cookies used to keep you signed in
What is not done

This demo does not sell or share your data with third parties, and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. It is a local/student deployment, not a production service.
